# 050 — Nega samolyot qanoti uchi qayrilgan? (vinglet)
- Smithsonian Air & Space, "How Things Work: Winglets": winglets reduce wingtip vortices formed by the pressure difference between the upper and lower wing surfaces; high pressure under the wing flows to the tip and curls upward around it; vortex streams behind; Whitcomb showed winglets reduced drag by about 20 % (induced drag): https://www.smithsonianmag.com/air-space-magazine/how-things-work-winglets-2468375/
- NASA Spinoff 2010, "Winglets Save Billions of Dollars in Fuel Costs": Richard Whitcomb (NASA Langley) published findings in 1976 (induced drag −≈20 %); KC-135 flight tests from 1977; blended winglets "typically produce a 4- to 6-percent fuel savings"; "a typical Southwest Boeing 737-700 airplane saves about 100,000 gallons of fuel each year" (≈378 500 L → "taxminan 380 000 litr"): https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/10666
- Aviation Partners Boeing press release, 9 Nov 2018 (manufacturer's estimate): blended winglets have saved an estimated 9 billion gallons of jet fuel (≈34 billion litres → "o‘ttiz milliard litrdan ortiq", labelled as the manufacturer's estimate): https://www.aviationpros.com/news/12436266/9-billion-gallons-of-jet-fuel-saved-95-million-tons-of-carbon-dioxide-eliminated
