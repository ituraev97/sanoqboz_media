# 024 — Elektr hisoblagich qanday hisoblaydi
- Aholi uchun tariflar 1.06.2026 dan: 200 kVt·soatgacha 650 so‘m, 201–500 — 900 so‘m, 501–1000 — 1 100 so‘m — https://gov.uz/oz/minenergy/news/view/165918 ; https://www.spot.uz/oz/2026/05/15/energy-tariffs/
- 1 kVt·soat = 1 kVt × 1 soat (fizik ta’rif); 3 200 imp/kVt·soat — elektron hisoblagich yorlig‘idagi odatiy qiymat, ekranda «misol»
- Hisoblar (o‘zimiz): choynak 2 kVt × 0,1 soat = 0,2 kVt·soat × 650 = 130 so‘m; isitgich 2 kVt × 8 soat × 30 kun = 480 kVt·soat; 200×650 + 280×900 = 130 000 + 252 000 = 382 000 so‘m (quvvatlar «misol»)
